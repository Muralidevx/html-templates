What this pathway decides

Given a patient with chronic knee pain, the pathway answers one question: is this patient a candidate for referral to a surgeon to evaluate total knee replacement — or should conservative care continue? The 2026 chart keeps that same shape but changes two things in how it gets there: what counts as evidence for the diagnosis, and what has to be documented before it will recommend referral.

Old pathway — 3 stepsChronic pain + DJD (stiffness must be absent) → adequate conservative trial (3 mo PT/NSAID/exercise, 6 wk others) → improved?
New pathway — 4 stepsChronic pain + DJD (stiffness supports it) → 6-week conservative trial, or intolerant/contraindicated → improved? → severity & functional-impact check

One thing the chart doesn't spell out — worth confirming with the clinical team rather than assuming an answer.

Severity gate: any-one-true, or all-four-true?

The severity & functional-impact question lists four criteria — pain most days, pain at rest/night, progressive functional decline, antalgic gait or assistive-device reliance. The chart doesn't say whether documenting one of these is enough to clear the gate, or whether all four need to be on record. This is a real fork: "any true" would refer most patients who've failed conservative care; "all true" would push a lot of them to "insufficient data" instead. Confirm the intended logic before encoding it as any_true vs all_true.
